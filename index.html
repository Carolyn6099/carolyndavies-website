What I offer

Counselling and coaching.
Not either/or.

The depth of counselling

Space to explore what's underneath — patterns, the past, the feelings you haven't quite been able to name. Unhurried. Without judgement.

The forward pull of coaching

When the time is right, we shift — clarifying what you want, what's in the way, and what the next move looks like. Your goals, your pace.

One relationship. Both directions.

Working within the Personal Consultancy Model means you don't need a therapist and a coach. We do both, because life rarely separates them.

Who I work with

People who are longing for change.

I work with individuals aged 18 and over — men, women, and people of all genders, including LGBTQ+ clients. You don't have to be in crisis. You just have to be curious about change.

The people I see often feel stuck — weighed down by anxiety or low mood, caught in patterns that keep repeating, or aware that something's missing but not sure where to start.

About Carolyn

Before this, I made television. Then I spent a decade teaching people to talk to each other.

I came to counselling through a long detour — years as a factual TV producer, followed by a decade training people in Compassionate Communication. Both worlds were about the same thing: helping people understand themselves and each other.

I'm BACP registered, hold a PGDip in Integrative Counselling and Coaching (with Distinction), and trained to Level 2 in IFS and as a certified NVC practitioner.
